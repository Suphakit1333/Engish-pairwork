Quick Grammar Review

📌 1. Comparative & Superlative

  • Comparative: Used to compare 2 things (keyword: than).
    • Short adjectives: Adj + -er than (e.g., bigger, faster)
    • Long adjectives (2+ syllables): more + Adj than (e.g., more difficult)
  • Superlative: Used to compare 3 or more things (always requires the).
    • Short adjectives: the + Adj + -est (e.g., the highest, the smartest)
    • Long adjectives: the most + Adj (e.g., the most expensive)
  • Irregular forms: bad → worse → the worst, good → better → the best

📌 2. Past Simple Tense

  • Affirmative: Subject + V.2
    (e.g., He lay under the tree. / The car cost a lot.)
  • Negative & Questions: Use did / didn't + bare infinitive (V.1)
    (e.g., Why didn't you contact...?)
  • Passive Voice in Past: was / were + V.3
    (e.g., Artifacts were stolen...)
  • Special Structure: It was (high) time + Subject + V.2
    (e.g., It was high time they took action.)
